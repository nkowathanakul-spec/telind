Three build notes

Figures are extracted and embedded. All 31 figures for these modules were pulled from the source PDF, paired with their own captions by page geometry, compressed and embedded directly in each page. Nothing external is needed — each file opens on its own. The chapter actually runs to Figure 1.36; all 36 are supplied as loose files in figures-extracted/.

Tables 1.1 and 1.2 are wrong in the source file. The image under “TABLE 1.1 Abdominal Wall Layers” is a lower-limb nerve injury table, and the image under “TABLE 1.2 Layers and Pouches of the Anterior Triangle” is an upper-limb nerve injury table. Both have been left out rather than shown under captions they do not match; the modules use tables reconstructed from the narrative instead, and say so.

Coverage stops at the rectovaginal space. The sections on the sacrospinous ligament region, the retroperitoneal spaces and lateral pelvic wall, structures above the pelvic brim, the presacral space and the pelvic lymphatics are not yet represented — that is where Figures 1.32–1.36 belong.
